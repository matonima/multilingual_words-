# Changelog

All notable changes to Words are recorded here. Versions follow semantic
versioning: patch for fixes, minor for backward-compatible features, and major
for incompatible changes.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.1.0] - 2026-08-08

- Corrected the Latin `aqua` guide and regenerated Sanskrit and Latin word
  clips using pronunciation-safe prompts.
- Redesigned language cards with bold identities, artwork, and clearer samples.
- Replaced the activity cards with visual alphabet characters and word tiles.
- Added the turquoise, ocean blue, mint, sunshine yellow, and hot-pink palette.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-08

- Initial multilingual Words application with Arabic, Russian, Sanskrit, and Latin.
- Split Arabic and Russian spelling into individual letters.
- Added Sanskrit vowel-kaar composition and pronunciation.
- Added repeat-until-drop and replay-pronunciation controls.
